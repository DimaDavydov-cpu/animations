Common terms to help you understand what everyone's talking about.

Gig
A service offered on Fiverr.

Marketplace, Fiverr Core.

service as a product- This is what Fiverr is all about, offering the purchasing experience of services as a complete single product, just like purchasing a product on ebay\amazon.
Delivery
Order that was delivered.
RNC
Registered Not Converted. User who registered to Fiverr but didn’t perform any other action.
