How to use

        

      

      

        
Card
Draw a rectangle or square with Pencil. One stroke or several, then pause. Every card is a stack: what you add inside flows vertically or horizontally with a Penta gap and padding.

        
Card properties
Tap a card with your finger. The panel shows direction, gap, padding, alignment, size (Fixed, Fill, Hug), fill, border, radius and elevation.

        
Nesting
Draw a rectangle inside a card to nest one. Draw a rectangle around existing items to wrap them in a new stack.

        
Text
Tap with Pencil where the text goes, then handwrite inside the box. iPadOS Scribble converts it. Pick H1, H2, H3, body… in the toolbar.

        
Edit text
Write over existing text with Pencil. Scratch out words to delete them (Scribble gestures).

        
Background
Tap the empty artboard with your finger to choose its color. Use the hex field for exact values.

        
Select / move
Finger tap selects. Drag an item to reorder it in its stack, into another card, or out onto the artboard. Corner handles resize. Two fingers pan.

        
Canvas width
Adaptive (default) uses the full available width. Drag the handle on the canvas's right edge to resize it, pick iPad or Mobile, or tap the width next to the scale (for example 1194 px) to type an exact width. Height is automatic.
Scale
Tap the percentage in the top bar to switch between 100%, 75% and 50%. Touch never changes the scale: two fingers only pan.
Grid snapping
Card edges snap to the layout grid's columns and margins when within 20 px. A red guide shows the line they snapped to.

        
Without Pencil
Mouse draws in Draw mode. Use Select mode (V) to select, move and pick the background color.
